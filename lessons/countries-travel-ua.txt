#Travel
country - країна | a nation with its own government and land
capital - столиця | the most important city in a country, where the government is located
flag - прапор | a piece of cloth with a special design that represents a country
city - місто | a large and important town where many people live
language - мова | the words and system of communication used by people of a country
famous - відомий | known and recognized by many people
beautiful - красивий | pleasing to look at and very attractive
interesting - цікавий | keeping your attention and making you want to know more
traditional food - традиційна їжа | food that a particular culture or country has made and eaten for a long time
landmark - визначне місце | an object or place that is easy to recognize and helps you know where you are
