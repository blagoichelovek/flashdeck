#Jobs
doctor - лікар | a person who is trained to treat sick or injured people
teacher - вчитель | a person whose job is to teach students, especially in a school
cook / chef - кухар | a person whose job is to prepare and cook food
police officer - поліцейський | a member of the police force whose job is to keep order and make people obey the law
firefighter - пожежник | a person whose job is to put out fires and save people from danger
vet - ветеринар | a doctor who treats sick or injured animals
dentist - стоматолог | a person whose job is to check and treat teeth
driver - водій | a person who drives a vehicle, such as a bus, car, or truck
artist - художник | a person who creates art, such as paintings or drawings
singer - співак / співачка | a person who sings, especially as a job
pilot - пілот | a person who operates the flying controls of an aircraft
astronaut - астронавт | a person who is trained to travel and work in space
