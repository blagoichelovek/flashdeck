#Jobs and professions (beginner)
doctor - лікар | a person who helps sick people get better
> This is a doctor. | Це лікар.
teacher - вчитель | a person who works in a school and helps students learn
> This is a teacher. | Це вчитель.
cook / chef - кухар | a person who makes food in a kitchen or restaurant
> This is a cook. // This is a chef. | Це кухар.
police officer - поліцейський | a person who keeps people safe and stops crime
> This is a police officer. | Це поліцейський.
firefighter - пожежник | a person who puts out fires
> This is a firefighter. | Це пожежник.
vet - ветеринар | a doctor who helps sick animals
> This is a vet. | Це ветеринар.
dentist - стоматолог | a doctor who looks after your teeth
> This is a dentist. | Це стоматолог.
driver - водій | a person who drives a bus, a car or a truck
> This is a driver. | Це водій.
artist - художник | a person who paints or draws pictures
> This is an artist. | Це художник.
singer - співак / співачка | a person who sings songs
> This is a singer. | Це співак.
pilot - пілот | a person who flies a plane
> This is a pilot. | Це пілот.
astronaut - астронавт | a person who travels into space
> This is an astronaut. | Це астронавт.
