# Sport
strength - сила | the power of your body or muscles
speed - швидкість | how fast something or someone moves
stamina - витривалість | the energy to do something hard for a long time
balance - рівновага | the ability to stay steady and not fall
coordination - координація | the ability to move different parts of your body well together
opponent - суперник | the person or team you play against
equipment - спорядження | the things you need for a sport or activity
protective gloves - захисні рукавички | gloves that keep your hands safe
warm up - розминатися | to do light exercise before a sport to prepare your body
practice - тренуватися / практикувати | to do something again and again to get better
improve - покращувати | to become better or make something better
compete - змагатися | to try to win against other people
perform - виконувати / виступати | to do a task, or to show a skill in front of people
achieve a goal - досягати мети | to succeed in getting something you wanted
